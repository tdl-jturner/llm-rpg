# Tone & Style

Dark, claustrophobic, and ancient. Every room should feel like it has been forgotten for
centuries. Describe stone, iron, torch-light, and shadow. Avoid modern language.
The dungeon has no mercy and no humour; it is simply vast and indifferent.

# Lore

Aethon was a mage-king who built this complex to house his experiments and his treasures.
He disappeared seven hundred years ago and no expedition has returned from the lower levels.
The dungeon is still populated by creatures of his making — amalgamations of flesh and old
magic that prowl the corridors looking for anything living.

# Visual Style

Stone is grey-green with moisture. Metal is orange with rust. Light sources are rare and
precious. Rooms should feel tight and low-ceilinged unless stated otherwise. Every room
should have at least one detail that suggests the passage of time — moss, rubble, collapsed
sections, ancient scratches in the stone.
