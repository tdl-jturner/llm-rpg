# Tone & Style

Lush, overgrown, and alive. The forest is not malevolent but it is not welcoming either;
it is simply vast and old and indifferent to human concerns. Describe plant life, animal
sounds, light and shadow, the smell of rain and earth. The danger here is not stone and iron
but tooth and root and the disorientation of a world that looks the same in every direction.

# Lore

The Verdant Labyrinth has no centre — or if it does, no one who reached it ever returned
to describe it. The trees grow in patterns that look almost intentional. Hunters enter and
are never seen again. Scholars theorise a sleeping intelligence beneath the roots, dreaming
the forest into new shapes each night. Whether this is true is left for the explorer to discover.

# Visual Style

Every room should feel organic and alive. Moss, fern, mushroom, stream, birdsong.
Monsters should be fauna — wolves, bears, animated root-things — never undead or mechanical.
Colours are green, brown, gold, and the dark blue of deep shade. Rain is frequent.
